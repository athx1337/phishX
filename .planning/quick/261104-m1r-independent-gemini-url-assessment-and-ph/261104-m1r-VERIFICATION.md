## Limits of this verification

The offline sample is small, selected, and positive-only. Its descriptive warning recall cannot establish precision, false-positive rate, general traffic accuracy, or a better model. The runtime artifact and threshold were frozen. The new Allegro domain rule is a response to inspected examples and should be measured separately from that baseline. The direct Gemini benchmark uses an opaque pretrained model, whose historical training overlap cannot be checked. URL-only review does not verify destination content. The refreshed hosted check verifies conservative behavior and fallback for the supplied example. It does not demonstrate a completed independent assessment for that URL. The broader direct-provider evaluation demonstrates actual risk signals and abstentions elsewhere, with substantial availability and recall limits disclosed in its per-ID results. No fine-tuning or XGBoost retraining occurred in this task.
